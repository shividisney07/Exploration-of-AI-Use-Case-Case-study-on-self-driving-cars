# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence to travel and perform driving tasks with reduced or no continuous human intervention, depending on their level of automation.

AI helps these vehicles understand road conditions, detect obstacles, recognise traffic signs, identify pedestrians, and make driving decisions.

The major AI technologies used in self-driving cars are:

1.Computer Vision: Identifies roads, traffic signals, pedestrians, vehicles, and road signs using cameras.

2.Machine Learning: Helps the vehicle learn patterns from driving data and improve object detection and prediction.

3.Deep Learning: Processes complex visual and sensor data to recognise objects and understand driving environments.

4.Sensor Fusion: Combines information from cameras, radar, LiDAR, GPS, and other sensors to build a better understanding of the surroundings.

5.Path Planning: Determines a suitable route and calculates a safe path to reach the destination.

6.Decision Making: Decides when to accelerate, brake, stop, or change lanes based on traffic conditions.

7.Control Systems: Convert driving decisions into steering, braking, and acceleration commands.

# WORKING PRINCIPLE

The working principle of a self-driving car involves the following steps:

1.Data Collection: Cameras and other sensors collect information about the vehicle's surroundings.

2.Environment Perception: Computer vision and deep learning identify vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, road markings, and obstacles.

3.Sensor Fusion: Data from multiple sensors are combined to obtain a more reliable representation of the environment.

4.Localisation: GPS, maps, and other sensor information help determine the vehicle's position.

5.Prediction: AI estimates the possible movements of nearby vehicles, pedestrians, and other road users.

6.Path Planning: The system selects a safe route and determines the vehicle's next movements.

7.Vehicle Control: Control systems adjust the steering, brakes, and acceleration to follow the planned path.

8.Continuous Monitoring: Sensors continuously update the environment information, allowing the vehicle to respond to changing road conditions.

# RESULT
Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The roles of computer vision, machine learning, sensor fusion, path planning, and control systems in enabling autonomous vehicles to perceive their surroundings, make driving decisions, and navigate roads were understood.
